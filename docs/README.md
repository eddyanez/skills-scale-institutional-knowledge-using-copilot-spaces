# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management guidance for cross-functional projects, from validating an idea through delivery, release, and continuous improvement. The process emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and open collaboration.

## Process summary

OctoAcme begins by validating the business need, intended outcomes, success measures, stakeholders, and team readiness. Once an initiative is approved, the team plans it as prioritized, shippable increments with clear acceptance criteria, ownership, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified risks and dependencies.

During execution, the team tracks work on a project board, communicates progress and blockers through regular team and stakeholder updates, and escalates issues when needed. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, plans, risks, and communication; the Product Manager/Product Lead defines outcomes and priorities; developers build and test changes; QA/testing validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Quality is integrated throughout delivery through unit and applicable integration tests, CI checks for tests and linting, security scanning, code review, and manual QA where needed. Releases require readiness checks, release notes, smoke tests, deployment verification, and a rollback or mitigation plan. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, the team captures learnings in retrospectives and tracks improvement actions with owners and due dates.
