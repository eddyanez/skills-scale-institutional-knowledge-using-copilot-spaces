# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers translate user needs into clear product experiences, usability insights, and design direction. They turn customer problems into intuitive flows and validate whether solutions are understandable, useful, and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design direction for user flows
- Define accessibility and interaction expectations
- Partner with Product Managers on feature priorities and customer pain points
- Work with Developers and QA to validate the final experience against acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and usability
- Reduce friction in key workflows
- Ensure design decisions match user needs and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Product feedback sessions and design reviews
- Usability findings and research summaries
- Collaboration in backlog refinement and acceptance criteria walkthroughs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: aligns on user needs, feature priorities, and success metrics
- With Developers: clarifies flows, edge cases, and implementation constraints
- With QA: validates interaction quality, accessibility, and acceptance criteria
- With Project Managers: shares design dependencies, milestones, and validation timelines

---

## Technical Lead or Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical direction, system design, and engineering standards. They help the team make sound decisions about architecture, complexity, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review technical architecture and design decisions
- Identify dependencies, integration risks, and long-term technical trade-offs
- Mentor engineers and support solution design reviews
- Align implementation choices with business goals and delivery constraints
- Partner with Project Managers to surface delivery risks and sequencing needs

### Goals
- Keep the solution maintainable and scalable
- Reduce avoidable architecture churn and technical debt
- Support efficient delivery without compromising quality

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency planning and risk updates
- Engineering decision logs and technical trade-off documents

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: helps assess feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery impact of ideas
- With Developers: reviews implementation quality, standards, and technical risks
- With QA: supports testability, observability, and automation strategy
- With Project Managers: escalates cross-team dependencies and technical blockers

---

## Operations / SRE or Release Owner

### Role Summary
Operations, SRE, or Release Owners ensure that products are reliable, observable, and ready to deploy. They focus on production readiness, release quality, monitoring, and recovery plans.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness and monitoring requirements
- Coordinate release windows, rollback readiness, and post-deploy checks
- Monitor system health, incidents, and recovery steps
- Help identify reliability risks before they reach production
- Partner with Developers and QA on smoke tests and deployment confidence

### Goals
- Minimize production risk and service disruption
- Improve observability and recovery time
- Support smooth, predictable releases

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Incident triage and operational status updates
- Post-deploy verification and rollback coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: aligns release timing, rollback planning, and escalation paths
- With Developers: validates production readiness, monitoring, and incident response requirements
- With QA: confirms smoke tests, release validation, and production confidence
- With Product Managers: communicates operational trade-offs and customer impact risks

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data or Analytics Leads define success measurement, instrumentation, and reporting so teams can understand whether the product is delivering the intended business or customer outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define key metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Plan instrumentation and event tracking across user journeys
- Validate data quality and the reliability of success measures
- Partner with Product Managers to evaluate whether outcomes are improving
- Work with Developers and QA to ensure reporting signals are measurable and trustworthy

### Goals
- Make impact measurable and understandable
- Improve decision-making with evidence-based insights
- Connect product work to customer and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Metrics reviews and dashboard updates
- Outcome measurement planning and analysis summaries
- Product health and experiment-readiness discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: translates goals into measurable outcomes and benchmarks
- With Developers: ensures instrumentation and data capture are built correctly
- With QA: validates metrics accuracy and reporting quality
- With Project Managers: shares status of measurement readiness and milestone dependencies

---

## Security / Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy partners review project work for risk, compliance, and trusted handling of data. They help the team build secure products without slowing delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Review architecture, data flows, and access controls for risk
- Recommend mitigations and review exceptions or controls
- Support secure release readiness and incident response planning
- Partner with QA and engineering teams on validation and control testing

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risk
- Maintain compliance with relevant requirements and standards
- Build trust with customers and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk assessments
- Privacy or compliance check-ins
- Release readiness and escalation discussions for critical findings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: ensures requirements reflect security and privacy needs
- With Developers: reviews implementation choices and mitigation planning
- With QA: validates controls and remediation effectiveness
- With Project Managers: aligns risk handling, escalation paths, and release gating

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Keep persona responsibilities aligned with the broader project lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective.
- When a role is not staffed as a separate person, the Team should assign a clear accountable owner so responsibilities remain visible and decision-making is not lost.
